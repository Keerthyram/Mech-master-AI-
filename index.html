WORKSHOP REFERENCE

Engineering tools

Vernier caliper

Measures external, internal and depth dimensions. Learn its parts, least count, reading method and care.

Micrometer screw gauge

Measures small dimensions using a calibrated screw mechanism. Study pitch, least count and zero error.

Dial indicator

Indicates small displacement, runout and alignment variation.

Lathe machine

Rotates a workpiece while a cutting tool removes material. Study turning, facing and threading concepts.

Milling machine

Uses a rotating multi-point cutter for machining flat and shaped surfaces.

Drilling machine

Produces holes using a rotating drill. Learn drilling operations and safe workshop practice.
